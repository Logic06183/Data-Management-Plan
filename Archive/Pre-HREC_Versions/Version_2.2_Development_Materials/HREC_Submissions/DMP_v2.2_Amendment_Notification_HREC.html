NATURE OF AMENDMENT: Temporary infrastructure transition during Year 5 to enhance data management capabilities through cloud infrastructure migration. This amendment documents a transition period where dual infrastructure capability (UCT on-premise and WHC-managed cloud) is maintained, with complete migration to cloud by end of Year 5. All existing ethical safeguards are maintained and enhanced.

PARTICIPANT IMPACT: No direct impact on participants. Enhanced data protection and security measures only.

RISK ASSESSMENT: Risk reduced through enhanced security, 24/7 monitoring, and automated backup capabilities.

ETHICAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT

Participant Rights: All existing participant rights maintained and enhanced through improved security infrastructure during transition period.

Data Protection: Significant enhancement through:

  • AES-256 encryption across all infrastructure
  • 24/7 professional security monitoring during transition
  • Automated backup and disaster recovery systems
  • Advanced threat detection capabilities
  • Dual infrastructure redundancy during transition

Consent and Transparency: Clear communication framework for data providers about transition. Emphasis on temporary nature of changes with defined endpoint.

Governance Enhancement: Universal DAC review requirement strengthens oversight. Consistent application of ethical standards to all data access requests.

Risk Mitigation: Transition period approach minimizes operational risks through:

  • Phased implementation over 12 months
  • Dual system operation for continuity
  • Enhanced security throughout transition
  • Professional monitoring and support

CONFIRMATION

These amendments document a temporary infrastructure transition during Year 5 that enhances data protection and research capabilities. The transition will be complete by end of Year 5 with full migration to secure cloud infrastructure. All existing ethical safeguards are maintained and enhanced. No changes to research methodology, participant recruitment, or data collection procedures.

The universal DAC review requirement ensures equitable and transparent data governance for all users.

All changes maintain full POPIA compliance and enhance alignment with international data protection standards.
