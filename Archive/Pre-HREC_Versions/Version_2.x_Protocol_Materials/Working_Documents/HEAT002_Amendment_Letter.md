**Letter of Amendment #2 dated 04 December 2023**

**Study Title:** Innovative machine learning and multi-source data
analysis towards development of an urban heat-health Early Warning
System for African cities

**Protocol number: HEAT002**

**Date of the last version of the protocol: 15 September 2023**

**HREC Reference No: 200606**

**The overall rationale for the amendment**

The amendments to the ethical considerations of the protocol stem from a
meeting with Professor Paul Ruff, chair of the ethics committee, where
insightful discussions and recommendations were provided.

**Implementation of the protocol modification**

Upon receiving the research ethics committee\'s approval for this
protocol amendment, the site would implement the protocol amendment
immediately. A copy of the protocol amendment approval and any
correspondence will be retained in the site's regulatory files of this
study.
