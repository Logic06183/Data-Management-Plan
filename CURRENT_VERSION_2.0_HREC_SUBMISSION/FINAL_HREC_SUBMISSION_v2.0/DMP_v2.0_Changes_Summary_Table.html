Data Management Plan Version 2.0 - Summary of Proposed Changes

Study Title: Heat and Health in Africa Transdisciplinary Center Data Management Plan

Protocol number: HE²AT-DMP-v2.0

Date of last version of protocol: 29 September 2025

HREC Reference No: RP1-220605 and RP2-220606

Overall Rationale for the Amendment

The HE²AT Center Data Management Plan Version 2.0 represents a comprehensive consolidation of infrastructure enhancements designed to transition our data management from UCT on-premise systems to WHC-managed cloud infrastructure. This strategic upgrade enhances data security, improves operational efficiency, and strengthens participant protections while maintaining full regulatory compliance and supporting the Center's expanded research capabilities in heat-health research across Africa.

Primary Strategic Objectives:

  1. Enhanced Infrastructure Security: Implementation of AES-256 encryption, 24/7 Security Operations Centre monitoring, and automated threat detection systems
  2. Strengthened Data Governance: Formal Data Access Committee processes ensuring consistent ethical oversight for all data access requests
  3. Improved Research Capabilities: Cloud-based computational resources with GPU acceleration enabling more sophisticated analyses
  4. Regulatory Compliance Enhancement: Strengthened POPIA compliance framework with automated monitoring and cross-border data transfer protections
  5. Operational Excellence: Automated backup systems, disaster recovery capabilities, and streamlined data management processes

Implementation of the Protocol Modification

Upon receiving research ethics committee approval for this protocol amendment, the HE²AT Center will implement the enhanced data management infrastructure through a structured approach that ensures continuity of operations and maintains the highest standards of participant data protection throughout the transition process.

Implementation Approach:

  • Phased Migration Strategy: Systematic transition with comprehensive testing and validation at each stage
  • Parallel Operations: Maintenance of existing systems during transition to prevent service interruptions
  • Risk Mitigation: Comprehensive rollback procedures and continuous monitoring to ensure data integrity
  • Stakeholder Communication: Regular updates to data providers, consortium partners, and regulatory bodies
  • Compliance Assurance: Continuous legal and regulatory review throughout implementation

A copy of the protocol amendment approval along with any correspondence will be retained in the HE²AT Center's regulatory files for this study, ensuring complete audit trail and compliance documentation.

POPIA Compliance Framework

Enhanced Compliance Standards: All data management enhancements in Version 2.0 maintain or significantly exceed existing POPIA compliance requirements:

  • Superior Encryption Standards: AES-256 encryption for all data at rest and in transit provides industry-leading protection exceeding previous security measures
  • Advanced Access Controls: Multi-factor authentication and role-based access controls with automated compliance monitoring and audit trails
  • Cross-border Data Protections: Where cloud infrastructure may be hosted outside South Africa, appropriate data transfer safeguards and compliance measures ensure continued POPIA compliance with enhanced monitoring
  • Enhanced Anonymization Procedures: All existing data anonymization procedures using participant IDs are maintained and enhanced with automated verification systems
  • Continuous Compliance Monitoring: Automated compliance reporting provides ongoing oversight, audit capabilities, and real-time compliance verification

Data Subject Rights Protection: According to the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (as amended), all enhancements provide stronger protection for data subjects through:

  • Enhanced technical safeguards with industry-standard AES-256 encryption
  • Continuous security monitoring through professional 24/7 Security Operations Centre
  • Improved access controls and multi-factor authentication mechanisms
  • Enhanced disaster recovery and data integrity protection systems
  • Automated compliance reporting ensuring continuous regulatory adherence

Phased Implementation Approach

Structured Migration Strategy ensures systematic enhancement while maintaining data integrity:

  1. Phase 1: Planning and Assessment - Complete infrastructure evaluation, finalize migration strategy, and establish baseline security assessments
  2. Phase 2: Pilot Implementation - Migrate limited, non-sensitive datasets for comprehensive testing, validation, and performance optimization
  3. Phase 3: Gradual Migration - Systematic transfer of datasets with parallel operations to ensure service continuity and data availability
  4. Phase 4: Full Migration - Complete transition of all sensitive health data to enhanced cloud infrastructure with comprehensive monitoring
  5. Phase 5: Optimization - Performance tuning, full utilization of enhanced capabilities, and ongoing improvement processes

Risk Mitigation and Quality Assurance:

  • Comprehensive risk assessment and rollback procedures at each phase
  • Continuous data integrity monitoring throughout the transition process
  • Regular stakeholder communication and regulatory compliance reporting
  • Independent validation and security assessment at each milestone

Conclusion and Participant Protection Assurance

The HE²AT Center Data Management Plan Version 2.0 represents a significant consolidation of infrastructure enhancements that strengthen data security, improve governance frameworks, and enhance operational capabilities while maintaining the highest standards of participant protection and regulatory compliance. These changes position the HE²AT Center to deliver on its research objectives with improved efficiency, enhanced security, and superior compliance monitoring, ultimately benefiting the broader research community and the African populations served by our heat-health research initiatives.

All enhancements strengthen rather than compromise participant protection through improved technical infrastructure, enhanced governance processes, superior compliance monitoring capabilities, and more robust data security measures that exceed current industry standards for health data management.
