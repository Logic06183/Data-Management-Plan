Study Title: Heat and Health in Africa Transdisciplinary Center Data Management Plan

Protocol number: HE²AT-DMP-v2.0

Date of last version of protocol: 15 August 2024

HREC Reference No: RP1-220605 and RP2-220606

Overall Rationale for the Amendment

The HE²AT Center Data Management Plan Version 2.0 represents a strategic enhancement of our data management infrastructure, transitioning from UCT on-premise systems to WHC-managed cloud infrastructure. This comprehensive upgrade is designed to enhance data security, improve scalability, and increase operational efficiency while maintaining full regulatory compliance and strengthening participant data protections.

Strategic Objectives of Version 2.0:

  • Enhanced Security Architecture: Implementation of AES-256 encryption, 24/7 Security Operations Centre monitoring, and advanced threat detection systems
  • Improved Data Governance: Establishment of formal Data Access Committee processes and streamlined institutional oversight
  • Scalable Infrastructure: Cloud-based computational resources with GPU acceleration for complex analyses and automated backup systems
  • Regulatory Compliance: Strengthened POPIA compliance framework with enhanced cross-border data transfer protections
  • Operational Excellence: Automated disaster recovery capabilities and compliance monitoring systems

Implementation of the Protocol Modification

Upon receiving research ethics committee approval for this protocol amendment, the HE²AT Center will implement the enhanced data management infrastructure through a structured 5-phase cloud migration approach during Year 5. This systematic implementation ensures:

  • Continuity of Operations: Parallel systems during transition to prevent data access interruptions
  • Risk Mitigation: Comprehensive testing and validation at each phase
  • Stakeholder Communication: Regular updates to data providers and consortium partners
  • Compliance Assurance: Continuous legal and regulatory review throughout implementation

A copy of the protocol amendment approval along with any correspondence will be retained in the HE²AT Center's regulatory files for this study.

POPIA Compliance

Enhanced Compliance Framework: All data management enhancements in Version 2.0 maintain or exceed existing POPIA compliance standards:

  • Enhanced Encryption: AES-256 encryption for all data at rest and in transit provides superior protection compared to previous standards
  • Access Controls: Multi-factor authentication and role-based access controls with automated compliance monitoring
  • Cross-border Transfers: Where cloud infrastructure may be hosted outside South Africa, appropriate data transfer protections and compliance measures ensure continued POPIA compliance
  • Anonymization: All existing data anonymization procedures using participant IDs are maintained and enhanced
  • Monitoring: Automated compliance reporting provides continuous oversight and audit capabilities

Data Subject Protections: According to the rules of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (as amended), all enhancements provide stronger protection for data subjects through:

  • Enhanced technical safeguards with AES-256 encryption
  • Continuous security monitoring through 24/7 SOC
  • Improved access controls and authentication mechanisms
  • Enhanced disaster recovery and data integrity protections

Implementation Approach

Structured 5-Phase Migration Strategy:

  1. Phase 1: Planning and Assessment - Complete infrastructure evaluation and finalize migration strategy
  2. Phase 2: Pilot Implementation - Migrate limited datasets for comprehensive testing and validation
  3. Phase 3: Gradual Migration - Systematic transfer of datasets with parallel operations to ensure continuity
  4. Phase 4: Full Migration - Complete transition of all sensitive health data to enhanced cloud infrastructure
  5. Phase 5: Optimization - Performance tuning and full utilization of cloud capabilities

Risk Mitigation: Each phase includes comprehensive risk assessment, rollback procedures, and continuous monitoring to ensure data integrity and regulatory compliance throughout the transition process.

Stakeholder Communication: Regular updates will be provided to all data providers, consortium partners, and relevant ethics committees throughout the implementation process.

Conclusion

The HE²AT Center Data Management Plan Version 2.0 represents a significant enhancement in data security, governance, and operational capabilities while maintaining the highest standards of participant protection and regulatory compliance. These changes position the HE²AT Center to deliver on its research objectives with improved efficiency and security, ultimately benefiting the broader research community and the populations served by our heat-health research initiatives.

All enhancements strengthen rather than compromise participant protection through improved technical infrastructure, enhanced governance processes, and superior compliance monitoring capabilities.
