## Purpose of Submission

We hereby submit for your review and approval a comprehensive amendment to the HE²AT Center Data Management Plan, updating from Version 1.0 (approved March 2023) to Version 2.0. This consolidated amendment represents strategic enhancements developed over the past two years of project implementation, now presented as a single, cohesive update for your consideration.

## Nature of Amendment

This amendment introduces significant enhancements to our data management infrastructure, security framework, and governance processes. While substantial in scope, these changes are designed to:

1. **Strengthen participant data protection** through enterprise-grade security measures
2. **Enhance regulatory compliance** particularly regarding POPIA and human subjects research requirements
3. **Improve research capabilities** through modern computational infrastructure
4. **Ensure sustainability** through scalable, resilient systems

## Key Points for Committee Consideration

### 1. No Impact on Participants
- No changes to recruitment, consent, or data collection procedures
- All enhancements strengthen existing protections
- Participant rights are maintained and enhanced
- No additional risks introduced to participants

### 2. Significantly Enhanced Data Protection
- Implementation of AES-256 encryption (exceeding industry standards)
- 24/7 professional security monitoring
- Geographic redundancy for disaster recovery
- Automated backup and recovery systems

### 3. Strengthened Governance
- Universal Data Access Committee review for all data requests
- Clear institutional accountability framework
- Comprehensive audit trails for all data operations
- Transparent decision-making processes

### 4. Systematic Implementation Approach
- Phased 10-month implementation plan
- Dual infrastructure during transition ensuring continuity
- Validation protocols at each phase
- Regular progress reporting to stakeholders

## Documentation Provided

We have prepared comprehensive documentation to facilitate your review:

1. **HREC Amendment Document** - Formal amendment in required three-section format with detailed change table
2. **Thematic Summary** - Changes organized by functional area for easier review
3. **Updated Data Management Plan** - Complete Version 2.0 document (available upon request)
4. **Risk Assessment** - Detailed analysis of risks and mitigation strategies (available upon request)

## Rationale for Consolidated Submission

We are presenting all enhancements as a single Version 2.0 update rather than multiple incremental amendments to:
- Provide the Committee with a comprehensive view of our data management evolution
- Demonstrate the strategic, coordinated nature of these improvements
- Minimize administrative burden through a single review process
- Ensure all changes are evaluated holistically for their combined impact

## Request for Approval

We respectfully request the Committee's approval of this Version 2.0 amendment. These enhancements position the HE²AT Center at the forefront of ethical health research data management while significantly strengthening participant protections beyond current standards.

We are committed to implementing these changes in full compliance with HREC requirements and welcome any questions or requests for clarification. We are available to present these changes to the Committee if required.

## Compliance Confirmation

We confirm that:
- All changes maintain or enhance existing ethical safeguards
- No reduction in participant protection levels occurs
- Full POPIA compliance is maintained throughout
- The research team has the capacity to implement these changes effectively
- Regular monitoring and reporting will ensure continued compliance
