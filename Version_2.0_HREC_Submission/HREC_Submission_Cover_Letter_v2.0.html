Dear Members of the Human Research Ethics Committee,

We respectfully submit for your review and approval the HE²AT Center Data Management Plan Version 2.0, which represents a comprehensive enhancement of our data management infrastructure and governance framework. This amendment demonstrates our continued commitment to maintaining the highest standards of participant protection while advancing our heat-health research capabilities.

Strategic Enhancement Overview

Version 2.0 implements a carefully planned transition from UCT on-premise infrastructure to WHC-managed cloud infrastructure, designed to:

  • Strengthen Data Security: Implementation of AES-256 encryption and 24/7 Security Operations Centre monitoring
  • Enhance Governance: Formal Data Access Committee processes and streamlined institutional oversight
  • Improve Research Capabilities: Cloud-based computational resources with GPU acceleration and automated backup systems
  • Maintain Regulatory Excellence: Enhanced POPIA compliance framework with strengthened cross-border data protections

Amendment Submission Components

This submission includes three key documents for your review:

  1. HE²AT Center Data Management Plan Version 2.0 with Track Changes: Complete document showing all modifications from the approved Version 1.0, clearly highlighting additions and deletions
  2. Summary of Changes for HREC Review: Detailed table documenting every change with location, description, and rationale, formatted according to HREC requirements
  3. This Cover Letter: Providing context and assurance regarding the strategic nature of these enhancements

Key Enhancement Categories

1. Infrastructure Security Enhancements

  • Transition from UCT on-premise to WHC-managed cloud infrastructure with superior security capabilities
  • Implementation of AES-256 encryption for all data at rest and in transit
  • 24/7 Security Operations Centre monitoring with advanced threat detection
  • Automated backup and disaster recovery systems with enhanced data integrity

2. Governance and Compliance Strengthening

  • Establishment of formal Data Access Committee processes for all data access requests
  • Enhanced POPIA compliance framework with automated monitoring and reporting
  • Streamlined institutional oversight with clear role definitions across UCT, IBM, and WHC
  • Additional legal and compliance review processes for ongoing oversight

3. Research Capability Enhancement

  • Cloud-based computational resources with GPU acceleration for complex analyses
  • Improved data integration capabilities through co-located health, climate, and socio-economic datasets
  • Enhanced analytical platforms with improved processing times and capabilities
  • Scalable infrastructure to support growing research demands

4. Operational Excellence Implementation

  • Structured 5-phase migration strategy with comprehensive risk mitigation
  • Automated compliance monitoring and reporting systems
  • Enhanced disaster recovery and business continuity capabilities
  • Streamlined data management processes with improved efficiency

Participant Protection and Compliance Assurance

We wish to emphasize that all enhancements in Version 2.0 strengthen rather than compromise participant protection:

  • POPIA Compliance: All changes maintain or exceed existing POPIA compliance standards, with enhanced cross-border data transfer protections where applicable
  • Data Security: AES-256 encryption and 24/7 monitoring provide superior protection compared to existing measures
  • Access Controls: Enhanced multi-factor authentication and role-based access controls with automated compliance monitoring
  • Governance Oversight: Formal Data Access Committee processes ensure consistent ethical oversight of all data access
  • Legal Review: Additional legal and compliance review processes provide ongoing oversight and assurance

Implementation Approach

The transition to Version 2.0 will be implemented through a carefully structured 5-phase approach during Year 5:

  1. Planning and Assessment: Complete infrastructure evaluation and migration strategy finalization
  2. Pilot Implementation: Limited dataset migration for comprehensive testing and validation
  3. Gradual Migration: Systematic transfer with parallel operations to ensure continuity
  4. Full Migration: Complete transition of all sensitive health data to enhanced infrastructure
  5. Optimization: Performance tuning and full utilization of enhanced capabilities

Each phase includes comprehensive risk assessment, rollback procedures, and continuous monitoring to ensure data integrity and regulatory compliance throughout the process.

Stakeholder Benefits

These enhancements provide significant benefits to all stakeholders:

  • Participants: Enhanced data protection through superior security measures and governance oversight
  • Researchers: Improved analytical capabilities and more efficient data access processes
  • Data Providers: Stronger security assurances and more transparent governance frameworks
  • Regulatory Bodies: Enhanced compliance monitoring and automated reporting capabilities
  • Research Community: More robust research infrastructure supporting high-quality heat-health research

Request for Approval

We respectfully request the Human Research Ethics Committee to review and approve this comprehensive enhancement to the HE²AT Center Data Management Plan. These changes represent a strategic investment in research infrastructure that will:

  • Strengthen participant data protection through enhanced technical safeguards
  • Improve research capabilities to better serve the HE²AT Center's mission
  • Ensure continued regulatory compliance with enhanced monitoring and reporting
  • Position the HE²AT Center as a leader in ethical and secure health data management

We are confident that Version 2.0 represents a significant advancement in our data management capabilities while maintaining the highest standards of ethical conduct and participant protection that the Committee expects.
