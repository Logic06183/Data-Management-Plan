**Letter of Amendment #1 dates 28 July 2023**

**Study Title: Individual Participant Data meta-analysis to quantify the
impact of high ambient temperatures on maternal and child health in
Africa**

**Protocol number: HEAT001**

**Date of last version of protocol: 05 May 2022**

**HREC Reference No: 200605**

**Overall rationale for the amendment**

The purpose of this study is to quantify the impact of heat on pregnant
women and children in sub-Saharan Africa using an individual participant
data meta-analysis approach. The overall rationale for the amendments
are to ensure that we update on all new staff that have joined the
project team, correct any typographical errors noted in the previous
draft on investigators institutions, and to improve the scientific
integrity and ethical considerations of the study. A summary of the
changes are as follows:

1.  Added new co-investigators and corrected institution names for study
    partners.

2.  Updated protocol registration details on PROSPERO.

3.  Changed date of inclusion for studies from 2000 to 2012

4.  Review of informed consent waivers by the Wits Human Research Ethics
    Committee instead of UCT Faculty of Science RE Committee to ensure
    specialised review for human health research ethics.

5.  Updated the table of variables to be more comprehensive.

**Implementation of the protocol modification**

Upon receiving research ethics committee approval for this protocol
amendment, the site would implement the protocol amendment immediately.
A copy of the protocol amendment approval along with any correspondence
will be retained in the site's regulatory files of this study.
